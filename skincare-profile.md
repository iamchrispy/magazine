## 4. Medicube AGE-R Booster Pro X2

Bought Oct 2026 (~$259–299). Six modes, levels 1–6, USB-C charging, AGE-R app over Bluetooth.

### Buttons
- **Power:** hold ⏻ for 2–3 seconds. It starts in Booster at Level 1.
- **Mode:** press MODE. The order is Booster → MC → Derma Shot → Dual → Mask → Air Shot.
- **Level:** briefly press ⏻ (1 to 6). **Always start at Level 1.**
- **Timer:** a voice announces each minute. It stops at 5 minutes (10 for Mask). Press ⏻ within 10 seconds to extend, or MODE for the next mode. Otherwise it turns off.
- **Voice language:** hold ⏻ and MODE together for 3 seconds.
- **App only:** LED colour changes, Dual Mode combinations, AI Care, volume, vibration and care reports.

### Modes (from the manual, plus my rules)
| Mode (default LED) | What it does | Manual frequency | How to use | My rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Booster X2** (red) | Pushes serum into skin | 1–3×/day, 5–10 min | Apply product generously, press firmly, roll from the inside of the face outward | ✅ Only over the **PDRN ampoule or hyaluron ampoule** |
| **MC X2** (green) | Microcurrent, plumping and temporary lift | 1–3×/day, 5–10 min | Over product, upward from inside to outside | ✅ Jawline and cheekbones. Good before events. |
| **Derma Shot X2** (orange) | EMS, definition | 1–3×/day, 5–10 min | Over product, pause 1–2 seconds on points | 🟡 Stronger EMS than the original, so stay at a low level |
| **Dual** (yellow) | Two modes at once. Default: top = Derma Shot, bottom = Booster. | 1–3×/day, 5–10 min | Over product, glide along skin | 🟡 Gentle serums only |
| **Mask** (purple) | Vibration to help a sheet mask absorb | Once a day, 5–20 min | **Only over a fabric sheet mask**, never bare skin | ✅ Plain hydrating masks. No acids or fragrance. |
| **Air Shot X2** (blue) | Pores and exfoliation | **2–3×/week max, 5 min**, with a 2–3 day rest between | **Clean, dry skin**, using the edge of the head, gliding lightly | 🟡 Counts as exfoliation, so **rest nights only**. Low level. Avoid the nose-dent scar. |

- **Order when combining modes:** Air Shot → Booster → MC → Derma Shot → Dual → Mask.
- **LED:** the colours are mode indicators: red, green, orange, yellow, purple, blue, and cyan (app only). Blue light can trigger pigment in my skin. The dose from a wand is small, but if Air Shot's blue bothers me, change its LED to red in the app.
- **Why only 2 nights a week when the manual says daily:** the device is fine daily, but Booster, Dual and Air Shot must not go over my actives. Optional extra on other days: **5 min of MC in the morning** over the hyaluron ampoule (cleanser → toner → hyaluron ampoule → MC → vitamin C → squalane cream → sunscreen).

### Never use it
- Over **retinol, Acreen, COSRX 15%, hydroquinone, or the vitamin C powder mix**
- On **eyeballs, eyelids, or the Adam's apple / front of the neck**
- On wounds, **inflamed acne**, dermatitis, itchy patches, sunburn, or areas with a recent procedure (e.g. **1–2 weeks after pico toning or TCA CROSS**, or as the clinic says)
- On the burn-scar patches (Air Shot and Derma Shot)
- Around the eyes, nose and mouth: **low level only**

### Care
- Wipe the head with a dry cloth or cotton pad after every use.
- Store it dry. The manual says to avoid humid bathrooms.
- It doesn't run while charging.
- **Travel:** carry-on only (lithium battery). Pack the USB-C cable and a Korea plug adapter (Type C/F, 220V). Bring the PDRN ampoule in a travel bottle.
- Stop and cool the skin if there's stinging, redness that lasts more than ~30 min, or bumps.

## 6. Separate issues (not part of the face routine)

### Arm burn scars (forearms)
- Flat, pink-tan patches: a mix of leftover redness and post-inflammatory pigment. Not raised.
- **Press test:** if the colour fades when pressed, it's redness (HQ won't help). If it stays, it's pigment.
- **AM:** Vita Ion-C solution (or the powder mix) → sunscreen on forearms daily, or long sleeves.
- **PM:** **MelaNosa 4% hydroquinone** (Dong-A) on brown areas only → moisturizer (PDRN cream or Plodica). Shelve MelaToning 2%; use only one.
- **HQ limit:** 3 months on, then 2–3 months off.
- Optional: silicone gel (Scaraway or Kelo-cote) for redness and texture.
- No visible change after 3 months → dermatologist (vascular laser for redness, fractional for texture).

### Nose-tip dent
- Atrophic scar from a high-school pimple I squeezed. Mature and fixed; products won't fill it.
- Best option: **TCA CROSS** (2–4 sessions). If deep or tethered: punch elevation or subcision. **No filler on the nose.**
- Prep skin with vitamin C for 2–4 weeks before; strict sunscreen after (PIH risk).

## 9. Key lessons
- Results come from **active ingredients at the right strength**, not brand prestige. Luxury creams are mostly very good moisturizers, and I already have plenty of moisture.
- Check where an active sits in the ingredient list, and whether the product stays on or washes off.
- Buy **normal sizes (30–50 mL)**, not salon sizes.
- On the next trip: scan → buy only the 1–2 products for my main issue → basics at Olive Young.
- Inju's approach is right: cheap single-ingredient actives, used consistently.
